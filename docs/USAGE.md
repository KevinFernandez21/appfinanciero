# Daily finance walkthrough

1. Unlock with your device credential. The lock is on by default; it is not database encryption.
2. On the empty dashboard, tap **Set up your first account**. Choose its name, ISO 4217 currency and opening balance (zero is allowed). First-run currency follows the device region when available; otherwise enter it explicitly. Later account, budget and goal forms suggest the first account currency; you can change it.
3. In **Accounts**, add a spending category and any other accounts you need.
4. In **Activity**, use **Record movement** for income, expenses or transfers. Amounts use the selected account currency. Expenses require a category; transfers require a different account in the same currency. The date defaults to today and can be edited as `YYYY-MM-DD`.
5. In **Plans**, add a category budget and a savings goal. Reserve an amount from an account for the goal. Reservations earmark existing money without moving balances; entering a new total updates the reservation.
6. Check **Dashboard** for balances and this month's income/expenses. Transfers are not income or expense, and reservations do not reduce account balances.

Amounts are exact: JPY accepts whole yen; USD/COP accept cents; KWD accepts three decimals. Excess fractional minor units are rejected, never rounded silently. Keep an encrypted backup and do not rely on this development build as your only financial record.
